5. How an Order works

5.1. Technical steps of concluding the contract:

  1. the User adds Products to the cart; an Order can be placed from a Product value of HUF 1,000;
  2. on the cart summary, the User reviews the Products, their prices, the Service Fee and the total to pay, and can change or delete anything before sending the order;
  3. the User selects the payment method (bank card or — where the app offers it — Apple Pay or Google Pay);
  4. the User sends the Order with the button labelled "Order with obligation to pay";
  5. the Platform confirms receipt of the Order without delay (5.4.);
  6. the Partner accepts or rejects the Order (5.5.).

5.2. Directly before the order button, the Platform displays clearly and prominently the main characteristics of the Products and the total to pay, including all fees.

5.3. These Terms are available in Hungarian and English; the language of the contract is the language in which the User uses the app (Hungarian or English). The contract is not a written contract. Drive & Go records the Order, and the User can view it later in their account, among their orders. Drive & Go has not subscribed to any code of conduct.

5.4. Confirmation. Drive & Go confirms receipt of the Order electronically without delay. It sends confirmation of the concluded contract on a durable medium (by e-mail), at the latest at handover; this confirmation also repeats the request and acknowledgement under point 8.3. If the confirmation of receipt does not arrive within 48 hours, the User is released from their offer.

5.5. Conclusion of the contract. The User's Order is an offer. The contract of sale is concluded when the Partner accepts it. If the Partner rejects the Order or does not accept it within 5 minutes, the Order ends and all amounts are returned under point 7.2. The User can withdraw the Order until the Partner accepts it (7.1.).

5.6. Preparation. The Partner prepares the Products after accepting the Order. The preparation time shown by the Platform is an estimate for information only.

5.7. Pickup instructions. The Platform shows the exact description of the Pickup Point (photo, written steps, stopping zone) after the Partner accepts; it can also be viewed before ordering with a separate button.

5.8. Pickup Window. The Pickup Window lasts at least 120 minutes from the Partner's acceptance, unless the Partner closes earlier: in that case it lasts until closing. If the Order is placed within 120 minutes before the Partner closes, the Platform shows the closing time before payment. No Order can be placed within the time set by the Partner before closing (default: 60 minutes).

5.9. Arrival. The Platform detects the User's arrival using location services; if this does not work, the User can use the "I'm here" button. The button may only be pressed when the User is actually at the Pickup Point; every press is logged.

5.10. Handover. The Partner brings the Products to the User's car and hands them over based on the licence plate provided. The Partner gives a receipt or invoice for the Product at handover.

5.11. Closing. The Order is closed when the Partner records the handover or the User records the pickup — whichever happens first. If neither records it, the Order closes automatically one hour after arrival, or when the User moves significantly away from the Pickup Point — whichever happens first.
